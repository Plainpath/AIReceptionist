Interactive prototype · v1

Quoting & invoicing
for Australian trades

White-labelled here as Hale Electrical. Three screens built deep — lead inbox, quote builder, client detail — with the PDF, money and setup screens wired enough to walk the whole flow.

Jump to a screen
Try these
Drag a price-book card up onto the quote to add a line.
Hold the mic and it parses a whole spoken line item into rows you confirm.
Quote a lead from the inbox → preview PDF → send → accept → invoice → record a part payment.
Assumptions
GST 10%, ABN on every document, AUD.
Leads arrive from the site chatbot and the AI secretary — the inbox is the home screen, money sits underneath it.
Quote shape is picked per quote: flat price, labour + materials, or itemised.
Every invoice chooses its own payment route: bank transfer on the PDF, or pay-now on the hosted link.
Theming reaches the PDF — brand name and accent are tweakable.
Next: Android parity pass, overdue reminder ladder (7/14/30 days), recurring jobs for the cleaning/landscaping segment, and a second PDF template.
